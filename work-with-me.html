Consulting

Work with me.

I take on software projects from requirements through deployment. I’ve delivered a commissioned geospatial application and built an offline field system with a web portal and native phone apps.

What I take on

Five kinds of project.

Domain software and GIS. I build software around the work your team already does. At a state forest department, I worked with senior officers to turn offline processes into applications. I also built JurisField for teams that need to assign work by area and collect evidence offline.AI agent systems. I build tools for teams that want agents to handle part of a workflow and people to review the result. I built an open-source workspace that gives coding agents separate Git worktrees and gives developers a place to inspect their changes. In Atlas, I built review points into the video production workflow.RAG and knowledge systems. I take on retrieval-augmented generation and knowledge projects where people need to check an answer against its sources. In Atlas, I built a research stage that brings documents and links into a project and lets the author review the material used to develop the script.Product engineering. I build web and native applications and can take responsibility for the backend and deployment. For JurisField, I built the web portal and Go backend as well as native iOS and Android clients, then tested the workflow on physical devices.Any other software project. I’m open to work outside these areas. My engineering work has included debugging a production C and C++ media server at Zoho and building Oriv on Apple’s system WebKit.

How it runs

From the people doing the work to deployment.

I start with the people doing the work and walk through the steps with them. We agree on what the first version needs to handle. I build a prototype we can review together, then take the agreed scope through testing and deployment. After delivery, I use feedback from the people using it to guide the next round of changes.

Who it suits

Teams who can bring their users to the table.

This works well if you lead a department, a company or an engineering team and can bring the people who’ll use the software into the discussion.
