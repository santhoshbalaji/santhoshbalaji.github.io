About

From compilers, to maps, to my own products.

I’m Santhosh Balaji S, a software developer and consultant in Chennai. I build GIS platforms and AI agent systems.

The path

Eight years, three kinds of work.

2019 to 2023, Zoho. Member of Technical Staff, working on compiler systems, collaboration software and platform architecture.

2024 to 2025, a forest department. Web developer on government workflows and operational systems. My recent work there was on forest fires: software that goes beyond a form and a chart, and uses geospatial analysis across many parameters to find patterns for monitoring and prevention.

2025 to now, founder. I design, build and run my own products: MapSmith, NammaTN, Oriv, JurisField and Atlas.

How I work

Agents write most of the code. I decide what ships.

Most of my code is written by AI agents that I direct and review. Between 2 and 13 November 2026 I ran more than 300 agent threads across half a dozen projects: a GIS platform, a browser, a civic app and a video pipeline.

Every change goes through review before it ships, and each project has its own tests and release checks.

Now

November 2026.

  • Building MapSmith, an enterprise GIS platform.
  • Shipping Oriv, a native browser for Mac and iPhone, at version 0.15.
  • Preparing my first video on what AI is doing to the software business.
