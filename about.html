About

From compilers and media servers, to maps, to my own products.

I’m Santhosh Balaji S, a software engineer and GIS consultant in Chennai. I build GIS platforms and AI agent systems.

The path

Since 2017, four kinds of work.

2017 to 2019, Zoho Schools of Learning. Two years of software training, eighteen months of it as a full-time intern inside Zoho engineering teams.

2019 to 2023, Zoho. Member of Technical Staff. I worked on compiler and programming-language frameworks, application platform engineering, and the C and C++ media server behind Zoho Meeting: audio, video and screen-sharing reliability, and recording for sessions with up to 50 video participants. I mentored five team members.

2024 to 2025, a forest department. Web developer at headquarters, working directly with senior forest officers. I modelled a computerisation programme of more than 20 modules, built applications, dashboards and workflows for work that had been done offline, and developed a GIS-based forest-fire prevention prototype.

2025 to now, independent. Software engineer, product engineer and GIS consultant. The fire-prevention prototype led to two commissioned geospatial projects: one delivered end to end, one in progress. Alongside that I build my own products: MapSmith, NammaTN, Oriv, JurisField and Atlas.

How I work

Agents write most of the code. I decide what ships.

I start with the people who will use the software, and I prefer to prove an idea with a working prototype before anyone commits to it.

Most of my code is written by AI agents that I direct and review, using Claude Code and OpenAI Codex. Between 2 and 13 November 2026 I ran more than 300 agent threads across half a dozen projects. JurisField, a field-operations system with a web app, a backend and native iOS and Android clients, reached its first end-to-end version in about a month.

I stay responsible for the architecture and for checking the result. Nothing ships without review, and each project has its own tests and release checks.

Beyond code

Policy, governance and maps.

I am in the final year of a B.A. in political science, sociology and English at Sikkim Manipal University, studied alongside my engineering work, and I hold a diploma in electronics and communication engineering.

That interest in how public institutions work is why much of what I build is for government, field teams and civic use. In August 2022 my four-person team won third prize at the Building Future Cities hackathon in Bengaluru.

  • Delivering a commissioned geospatial application.
  • Shipping Oriv, a native browser for Mac and iPhone, at version 0.15.
  • Preparing my first video on what AI is doing to the software business.
