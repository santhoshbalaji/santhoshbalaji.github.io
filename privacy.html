Measurement

What is measured.

Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate page views, visits, referring sites, broad country, device category, browser, operating system and page-performance figures. They show whether the site works well and which pages are useful.

A small Cloudflare beacon is loaded only on santhoshbalaji.cloud. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not use client-side state or fingerprint individuals. Read Cloudflare’s Web Analytics documentation ↗.

Your choice

How to opt out.

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, this site does not load the analytics beacon. You can also block the beacon with browser privacy tools without losing any part of the site.

Not collected

What is not collected.

The site sets no analytics cookies and stores no analytics identifiers in your browser. Its analytics do not collect names, email addresses, precise location, full query strings or interaction histories, and are not used for advertising, profiling or remarketing.

Contact form

What the form keeps.

If you send the form on the Services page, your name, email address, message and timeline are stored on a server I run, so that I can read and answer them. They are not shared with anyone and are not used for marketing. To limit abuse, the server keeps a scrambled marker of the sender that changes every day; it does not store your IP address.

Email me if you want your message deleted.

Retention

How long data is kept.

Cloudflare currently retains unsampled beacon data for seven days and makes aggregate reports available for the previous six months. This site keeps no analytics database of its own and no visitor-level exports.
